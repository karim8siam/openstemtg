## 1. Golden Rules of University Partnerships Outreach

1. **Never Sound Like a Spam Broker:** University international directors receive dozens of generic emails asking "Please send agent contract with 15% commission". Always lead with OpenSTEM's academic ecosystem, student quality, and compliance safeguards.
2. **Personalize Every Email:** Reference the specific university name, key STEM faculties (e.g., College of Engineering, School of Computing), and their current international student requirements.
3. **Keep the Initial Ask Low-Friction:** In the first email, do not demand a signed contract. Ask for their **institutional agent application questionnaire** or invite an introductory 15-minute introductory video call.
4. **Attach the Institutional Profile:** Always attach or link the *OpenSTEM Institutional Information Profile* PDF for immediate administrative review.
5. **Use Professional Tracking:** Log every outreach attempt in the pipeline tool to ensure follow-ups occur on a disciplined schedule (Day 1, Day 5, Day 12, Day 20).

## 4. Handling Common University Objections

### Objection 1: *"We are not appointing new agents in Bangladesh at this time."*
**Winning Response:**
> *"Thank you for the update, [Name]. We completely understand that your agent roster for Bangladesh is currently capped. Could we be placed on your waitlist for the next review cycle? In the interim, would your office permit OpenSTEM to refer prospective students directly through your standard international admissions portal without a commission agreement, so our students can still consider [University Name]?"*  
*(Note: Referring 1-2 students directly builds goodwill and almost always opens the door to a contract for the next cycle!)*

### Objection 2: *"We only work through master aggregators (e.g., INTO, ApplyBoard, Shorelight)."*
**Winning Response:**
> *"Thank you for clarifying your recruitment route. We are active partners / currently onboarding with [Name of Aggregator, e.g. INTO / ApplyBoard]. Could you confirm your institution's specific profile or campus ID within that platform so our advisors can correctly map our applicants to [University Name]?"*

### Objection 3: *"Please send your trade license, certificate of incorporation, and 3 university references."*
**Winning Response:**
> *"Thank you for outlining your due diligence requirements. We are attaching our formal Institutional Information Profile, founder identification, and company status declaration. Our Bangladesh municipal trade license and tax registrations are on file and can be countersigned by our legal counsel. As we are currently expanding our direct bilateral university agreements in [Country], we would be pleased to provide academic endorsements and character references from secondary school principals and STEM faculty leads in our network."*
