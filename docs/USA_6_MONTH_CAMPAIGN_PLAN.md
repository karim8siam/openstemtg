## 1. Campaign Mission & 6-Month Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The United States represents the largest, most profitable, and most STEM-receptive international education market in the world. With **3-year STEM OPT post-graduation work rights**, high demand in Bangladesh, and generous 10%–15% tuition commissions, this 6-month campaign establishes OpenSTEM as a recognized U.S. recruitment authority.

### 📅 Month 3: Due Diligence, Compliance Clearance & Agreement Execution
* **Objectives:**
  1. Process incoming agency questionnaires, reference checks, and commission schedules.
  2. Countersign your first 15–20 direct U.S. representation contracts.
* **Key Actions:**
  * Complete university compliance packets using the pre-formulated responses in [`docs/COMPLIANCE_AND_DUE_DILIGENCE.md`](file:///Users/karimsiam/Downloads/open%20stem%20university%20outreach/docs/COMPLIANCE_AND_DUE_DILIGENCE.md).
  * Attach your finalized Dhaka e-Trade License and corporate bank wire details for incoming SWIFT tuition disbursements.
  * Review commission terms (standard: 10%–15% tuition for the first academic year / 2 terms; ensure payment triggers on the university census date).

---

### 📅 Month 4: Agent Onboarding, Portal Access & Dhaka STEM Student Funnel
* **Objectives:**
  1. Receive official university agent portal logins and attend institution partner webinars.
  2. Activate OpenSTEM's student recruitment funnel in Dhaka for U.S. admissions.
* **Key Actions:**
  * Complete designated university partner training modules and obtain marketing collateral.
  * Host virtual or physical OpenSTEM seminars in Dhaka: *"Demystifying U.S. STEM Degrees, Scholarships & 3-Year STEM OPT"*.
  * Guide prospective students on using [OpenSTEM Lab](https://openstemlab.org/) and [OpenSTEM Replicate](https://openstemreplicate.org/) to create academic portfolios for U.S. admissions.

---

### 📅 Month 5: Live Admissions Processing & Visa Safeguards
* **Objectives:**
  1. Submit 25 to 50 complete, pre-vetted student applications directly through partner portals.
  2. Guide admitted students through I-20 issuance and rigorous F-1 visa preparation.
* **Key Actions:**
  * Verify student transcripts, financial solvency (bank statements & tax records), and English test scores (IELTS / TOEFL / Duolingo) prior to submission.
  * Submit student files to partner universities; track application status and expedite I-20 processing.
  * Conduct mock visa interviews with applicants, testing academic intent and strong home-country ties to minimize visa refusals.

---

### 📅 Month 6: Enrolment Confirmation, Commission Invoicing & Transition
* **Objectives:**
  1. Confirm student enrollments past the university tuition refund / census date.
  2. Submit initial commission invoices to partner university finance offices.
  3. Seamlessly transition the outreach engine to **Country #2: United Kingdom**.
* **Key Actions:**
  * Reconcile student arrivals with university international admissions teams.
  * Generate and submit formal commission invoices per signed representation contracts.
  * Compile the finalized U.S. Partner Portfolio directory for OpenSTEM marketing.
  * Re-tool [`tools/pipeline_manager.py`](file:///Users/karimsiam/Downloads/open%20stem%20university%20outreach/tools/pipeline_manager.py) to launch the 6-Month Sprint for **Country #2: United Kingdom (163 universities)**.
